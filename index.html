Privacy & limitations

Typed dialogue and notes stay in this browser’s local storage. Do not enter real patient information. Optional browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser provider; text input works without it. Portraits are AI-generated fictional people and do not demonstrate physical signs.

This static app uses a scripted patient engine, not a live language model. It is not endorsed by the NHS or MRCP(UK). A browser cannot guarantee audible bells while a device sleeps or the page is suspended; the clock catches up on return. Keep this tab visible and the device awake.
